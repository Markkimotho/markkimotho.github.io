Where it started

Some of my earliest experiments with a computer were a meal-planning menu made in Word and afternoons spent building cities in SimCity. I still like figuring out how a system works, then trying to make one of my own.

I also spent a few years trying dropshipping. It did not become a successful business, but it taught me resilience. One part I still value is learning how to set up a US LLC and seeing how much legal and payment infrastructure matters when starting a business. I kept what I learned in a dropshipping guide.

Hadithi is a small, joyful step in my work to tell African stories through games. Its opening adventure, _The Wisdom in the Pot_, reimagines a Fante Anansi tale in present-day Ghana. Three other journeys draw on Japanese, German, and Baghdad storytelling traditions.

Explore each world, gather clues, make choices, and roll dice with honest odds. The story remembers what you learn, so a return journey can reveal a path you could not see before.

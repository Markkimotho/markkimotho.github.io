A small Python wrapper around the system's `traceroute` command. It validates the target, bounds the hop count and wait time, and passes arguments without invoking a shell.

This runs on a computer with `traceroute` installed. It is a local network diagnostic, not a browser app or a website-safety check. There is no public demo yet.

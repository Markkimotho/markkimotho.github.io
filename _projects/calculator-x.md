Calculator X started as a keyboard-friendly arithmetic calculator. It has grown into 17 small tools for the questions that come up during a normal week: whether to pay someone to do a task, what a subscription really costs over time, how to split a dinner bill, or whether a piece of furniture will make it around a corner.

Each calculator has its own page and explains the assumptions behind its result. The original [arithmetic calculator](https://calculator-x-qcvh.onrender.com/arithmetic/) is still there too. Try one and tell me which question I should turn into a calculator next.

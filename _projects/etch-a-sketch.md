A browser-based sketch pad inspired by the Etch A Sketch. Move across the grid to draw, pick a color or let the app choose one, erase squares, and clear the pad when you want to start over.

The grid-size slider rebuilds the canvas from 1 × 1 up to 100 × 100 squares. It is a small project, but a good excuse to work directly with DOM events and CSS grid.

Enter a domain to see its public DNS records and, when the registry allows browser access, its registration date. The app uses Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS and RDAP. Neither a registration date nor a DNS record can establish that a site is legitimate; the tool deliberately avoids giving a scam verdict.
